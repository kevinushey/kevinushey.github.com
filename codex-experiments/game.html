SURVIVAL, BRIEFLY

Your suit has a few tricks.

Find the gold access card on each deck and defeat its portal guardian, then walk through the glowing transit portal inside the lift frame. There is no final floor. Reach each lift alive to score one completed floor, then choose a permanent upgrade and resupply. You can also use E or the touch Use button near the portal. Enemy squads grow tougher, faster, and more numerous until the station overwhelms you. Explore side rooms for supplies. Your high score is floors completed; Standard and Explorer keep separate records on this device.

The pulse rifle holds 24 rounds; your suit carries at most two spare magazines (48 rounds), for 72 total. Start with one spare. Each lift supplies just 24 rounds. Ammo is scarce: check side rooms, conserve shots, and use the cutlass. Excess pickup ammo stays on the floor.

Each floor locks down after one minute. Every 30 seconds after that, surviving enemies gain health, damage, speed, and aggression. The lift stays available. The timer pauses with the game and tactical map, and resets at the next floor.

Two violet breach nodes manufacture reinforcements throughout each floor, faster during lockdown. A glowing marker warns you before a machine arrives. Shoot or slash the floating energy core to seal a breach permanently; EMP cancels its current spawn and disables it for eight seconds. These projections and their floor pads are passable. Spawned machines carry no spare ammo. Sealing breaches is optional; it stops production but cannot stop lockdown.

Three guardians alternate between floors: the Bastion opens its frontal armor to fire paired cannon bolts, the Prism Sovereign casts wide volleys you can dodge or parry, and the Rift Reaver charges along a telegraphed line. All become more dangerous below half health and strengthen during lockdown. Guardians resist blade stagger, but EMP interrupts their attacks for 1.4 seconds. They stay in the lift room and retain damage if you retreat. Defeating one releases the portal seal; you still need the access card.

Each floor has an optional contract: scrap machines, land cutlass kills, or salvage crew caches. Complete it for a second permanent upgrade at the lift. You can leave without it at any time. Arc welder upgrades your blade and reflected bolts; choose a build that suits your run.

NOW TRANSMITTING
ION RUNNER · Four original 8-bit space scores
Pulse leads, triangle bass, drifting pads, noise drums. New music on each floor; combat and extraction intensify the arrangement.

Your shield regenerates after five seconds without damage. A red arc around the crosshair points toward the last incoming hit, including hits absorbed by your shield. EMP stuns nearby machines and clears incoming bolts. Chain three blade swings for a heavy finisher; swing at incoming bolts to send them back. The blade ignores frontal armor. Bulwarks resist rifle shots from the front—flank them or use EMP. Swift, reinforced, and overclocked machines trade speed, durability, and firing rate. Red eyes mean a shot is coming—keep moving.
