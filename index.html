A little background

From statistics
to software.

I like building tools that help researchers and scientists answer the questions they care about.

At Posit, formerly RStudio, I work on open-source software for data science, from development tools to package management and reproducible R environments.

Before that, I was a statistical programmer in the Gottardo Lab at Fred Hutchinson Cancer Research Center.

I earned my MSc in Statistics at the University of British Columbia, working with Jenny Bryan. My research explored gene–gene and gene–drug interactions in yeast growth curves.
